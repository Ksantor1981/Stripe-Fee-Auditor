# Distribution copy (PH / IH / social)

**Rule (Jul 2026):** never write "your data never leaves your browser" — CSV is sent to the server for analysis.

## Honest privacy line (use everywhere)

> No OAuth. Raw CSV is not stored; we keep only redacted calculated results.

Longer:

> No Stripe OAuth or API keys. You upload a Balance CSV; we analyze it on the server in memory, do not keep the raw file, and store only a redacted calculated report (see Privacy Policy).

## Tagline / positioning

- **Not:** "Audit your Stripe fees"
- **Yes:** "Find why your Stripe effective rate (or payout) is higher than expected"
- Pain hooks: international card fees · refund fees not returned · monthly rate drift / anomalies

## Fee Monitor (not "another monthly report")

> Catch rate drift, refund leakage, and one-off anomalies before they become normal.

## Product Hunt / Indie Hackers — short blurb

```
Stripe shows 2.9% — your effective rate is often higher.

Upload a Balance CSV → see international cards, refund fee leakage, and monthly anomalies.
Mark one-off weird charges to compare your normal rate vs outlier-adjusted rate.

No OAuth. Raw CSV is not stored; we keep only redacted calculated results.
Free preview → https://feeauditor.com/analyze
```
